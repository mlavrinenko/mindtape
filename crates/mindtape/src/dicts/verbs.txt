# Method: keep a frequency-ordered word only when it appears in the forms dictionary

# and `made` drop out), drop the modals `can`/`will`/`would` (no imperative reading),
# then take the first 500. Entries are lowercase; a dict with `case = "fold"` (the
# default) matches a capitalised title word against them.
#
