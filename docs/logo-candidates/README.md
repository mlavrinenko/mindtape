# Logo candidates — review only

Three further directions for the MindTape mark, produced alongside the
shipped cassette (`www/assets/images/logo.svg`, untouched). None of these
are wired anywhere; they exist for side-by-side comparison only.

Shared constraints, matched to the shipped mark's house style: `viewBox
"0 0 24 24"`, single colour `#12805a`, no gradients/filters/opacity, and
meaning carried in the outline and in negative space — never in a contrast
between two fills, since the site header flattens the file to a CSS mask.

## Files

- `graph-in-cassette.svg`, `tape-waveform.svg`, `reel-checkmark.svg` — the
  three candidates.
- `contact-sheet.svg` / `contact-sheet.png` — all three at 96px, 26px and
  16px, on a white (`#ffffff`) and a dark (`#14171a`) panel. The sheet is
  generated by inlining each candidate's actual `<path>` data into `<symbol>`
  defs and `<use>`-ing them at size, so it can never drift from the shipped
  files.

## graph-in-cassette

Same lid-plus-body cassette silhouette as the shipped mark, but the window
is cut as one hole (negative space) and a small root-with-two-children
graph sits inside it: two paths, both `#12805a` — an evenodd path for the
cassette + window, and a second, non-overlapping-safe path (default nonzero
winding, everything wound the same direction) for the three node circles
and their two thick connecting bars, so the edges union into the nodes
instead of punching holes where they meet.

Nodes are 1.8r and edges are a bold quadrilateral — deliberately oversized
after an earlier attempt made both too small and the edges collapsed into a
squiggle. At 96px+ it reads unambiguously as a 3-node dependency graph
(nods to `mt graph`). At 26px the three nodes are still visible as rounded
bumps at the corners of a "peaked" shape. At 16px it reads clearly as a
cassette with a textured window, but the specific node-and-edge reading is
soft — it compresses toward a triangular/tent silhouette rather than three
distinct dots. Honest trade-off: treat 16px as "cassette, has detail
inside" and rely on 26px+ (favicon, larger touch icon) for the graph to
read explicitly.

## tape-waveform

Five stadium-capped vertical bars of varying height (6, 12, 16, 12, 8
units), one path, no holes needed at all — the meaning is entirely in the
bar heights and the gaps between them, which is the most literal reading
of "meaning lives in outline and negative space" of the three. Reads
simultaneously as a magnetic-tape level meter and a bar chart.

This is the most robust candidate at small sizes: distinct bars survive
down to 16px with no compromise, because there's no nested detail — every
element is already at top level. Trade-off: it drops the cassette shape
entirely, so it reads as "tape/audio" more than "task tracker," and it has
the least connection to `mt`'s other affordances (graph, checklist).

## reel-checkmark

A tape reel (outer ring, hub hole cut as negative space) sits upper-left;
its unspooled tape is a single ribbon, pointed at both ends, that bends
through the same silhouette as a checkmark. One evenodd path: the ring and
the ribbon are positioned so they never overlap, so the hub stays the only
hole and the ribbon's polarity doesn't matter.

At 96px this reads cleanly as reel-plus-check. At 26px both halves are
still legible. At 16px the ring is small enough that, out of context, it
could pass for a plain dot or "o" rather than an obvious spool — the
checkmark tail is the stronger element at that size. Trade-off: it is the
candidate most dependent on being seen at 26px+ (or with the reel/hub
detail nudged larger) to read as "reel" specifically rather than just
"circle with a tick."

## Recommendation

`tape-waveform` is the safest pick purely on legibility — it has no
size-dependent compromise. `graph-in-cassette` is the most thematically
on-brand (keeps the shipped cassette shape and ties directly to `mt
graph`) and is the one I'd pick if the icon is mostly seen at 24px+; it
needs a size floor to fully land. `reel-checkmark` is the most conceptually
clever (unspooling tape *becomes* the checkmark) but reads weakest at the
smallest sizes among the three. If forced to ship one today: `tape-waveform`
for uncompromising legibility, `graph-in-cassette` for brand continuity.

## Verification

Every candidate was rendered with `rsvg-convert` (fails loudly on
malformed XML — that was the validity check) at 16/26/96px, plus a
black-fill copy at 16/26px to confirm the flat-silhouette (CSS-mask)
reading holds. All PNGs were inspected directly, not assumed; the
`graph-in-cassette` window/node geometry went through two iterations
before the nodes read as distinct at 26px+.
